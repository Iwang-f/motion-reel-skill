## Cold read, first run

One `sonnet` reader per film, the script alone, no picture. Readers, not an audience: this finds faults, it measures nothing. Asked with the first version of `prompts/cold-read.txt`.

| | `byan-82` | `anda-ipo-80` | `tmpo-85` |
|---|---|---|---|
| SCROLL | stays, nearly left 0:12–0:29 | leaves 0:37–0:45 | stays, nearly left 0:13–0:30 |
| YOUR PART | the closing ask only | l01, l08, l18 | the closing ask only |
| REPEAT | l06, the line already flagged as a fact risk | l08, the prediction | l21, the disclaimer |
| LAST | no So what | "cek siapa pemilik sebenarnya sebelum ikut IPO" | "belum tahu mengapa itu penting" |
| REPORT VOICE | 6 of 12 lines | 8 of 20 | 10 of 22 |

- The old pass line would have passed BYAN and TMPO and failed ANDA: SCROLL ran backwards, since a reader owes a script nothing and stays. YOUR PART (once the closing ask is excluded), REPEAT and LAST told the films apart, so the pass line now rests on them.
- All three sagged on names and figures, jargon, and a product line with no reason to go.
- ANDA's 35 + 60 beside `70 persen sisanya` read as wrong. `Sebelum masuk tambang, baca papan tagnya` read as lost, partly because a reader has no picture.
- **BYAN as heard.** The reader took away "apakah ini kebetulan atau terkoordinasi", "istri Menteri Pertanian yang diam-diam muncul", and "kepemilikan Bayan berpindah ke lingkaran Haji Isam, dan ada nama pejabat di dalamnya". The film's own limits rule out implied coordination and a link to office. One LLM reader, not a legal opinion; it is a question for the legal review.
