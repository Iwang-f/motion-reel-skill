# Retro, last five films (30 Okt 2026)

Ten sessions: `mi-demo-40`, `check-email-16`, `aspi-55`, `andi-55`, `satu-persen-58`, each a pre-production and a build. Read
from the session logs. "Units" are input-token equivalents (cache read 0.1, cache write 1.25, output 5), for comparing
between sessions, not a bill.

Cache reads, by what was being re-read: base context 36 %, the model's own retained output 32 %, tool results 32 %.

- **Base context, about 95k tokens at every turn.** Tool schemas 128k chars, deferred tool names 44k, skill list 41k, system 34k,
  CLAUDE.md and vault imports 22k, MCP instructions 9k, agent list 7k, hook output 11k.
- **Late turns cost three times early ones.** Context ran from 95k to 230–515k, and pre-production wrote its whole `film.js` at
  214–353k. A simulated reset near 220–250k saves 17–18 % (8.1–8.3M), counting 135k to restart.
- **Thinking is about 77 % of output** (1.2M tokens estimated) and stays in context.
- **The tail after the last round** cost 2.0–2.3M on `mi-demo-40` and `satu-persen-58`, and 4.4M on `aspi-55` with its revision:
  39–75 turns at 320–480k for one master, its checks and the fixes they forced.
- **Reading the previous film** took 14–55k chars a session, though films share only 3–16 % of their code.
- **Two thirds of messages carried a single tool** (1.2–1.6 tools a message; 2.45 on `satu-persen-58` build).
- **Images were not the problem:** about 3 % of cache reads, despite the rules about them.

## Where the time went

- **The hang.** `satu-persen-58`: four workers on the 8 GB machine stopped at 10 % CPU with no error for 356 min, until stopped.
- **Masters.** 1–6 master commands a film, each 3–18 min. Reasons: logo pixels found after the render, Market Intel moved,
  owner revisions, the hang.
- **Whole-film determinism.** 13 `--hash-only` runs across three films, each as long as a master, while `check.mjs` already
  samples it every round. `critique-pass.txt` asked for them.
- **Rounds:** 1–10 min each, growing with the film's weight. Every film reached the cap of four. Round-one density was 4, 4, 4, 5, 5: density was patched in
  afterwards, including decoration added to pass the number (`satu-persen-58`, round 1).
- **VO re-generation.** `mi-demo-40` paid three times (about 2,474 credits): language and tickers moved after the first take.
- **Not bottlenecks:** the 99 min of Edit time on `satu-persen-58` is an artefact of parallel batches; `film.js` boilerplate
  is too small for a template to pay by itself.

## Quality

The critique scored motion only: hook meant "5+ moves", and no axis asked why a stranger would watch. Round-one hook scores were
8, 6, 6, 7, 6. The reels study (`REFERENCES/motion/reels-study-2026-10.md`) points the same way: the reels the owner admires
rest on stake, story and pace, and all of them fail our density gate.

Every draft ran over the word budget: ANDI about 126 against 81, ASPI about 150 against 81, `satu-persen-58` about 146 against
86. At the reels' pace (2.6–3.0 words a second) those drafts fit.
